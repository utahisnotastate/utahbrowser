# Utah Browser — guide for kids

Hi! This page is for **you**, your family, and anyone who wants the simple story.

**Project:** [github.com/utahisnotastate/utahbrowser](https://github.com/utahisnotastate/utahbrowser)

---

## What is Utah Browser?

A **web browser** on your computer — like other browsers, but built to keep more of your stuff **private** on your machine.

**Truth Guard** is a helper inside the browser. It compares things you read online to **notebooks** (your family’s saved notes and files) and says whether they seem to match.

Think: *“Does this match my notebook?”*

---

## What you see on screen

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Utah Browser    [ web address ]  [ Go ]    │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Tools │   the website you are visiting      │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
              Truth HUD ← messages pop up here
```

- **Address bar** — where you want to go, then **Go**
- **Tools** — Truth Guard buttons
- **Truth HUD** — fact-check results

The layout **resizes** on big monitors, small laptops, and tablets in landscape.

---

## How to use Truth Guard

1. A grown-up runs the installer once ([easy guide for grown-ups](FOR_EVERYONE.md)).
2. Open **Tools**.
3. Tap **Ingest Notebooks** so the browser learns your family’s files.
4. Paste a sentence into **Verify statement**.
5. Tap **Verify**.
6. Read the answer in **Truth HUD**.

Green-ish often means *matches your notebooks*. Warning colors mean *double-check with a grown-up*.

---

## Stay safe online

- Ask a **parent or teacher** before new websites.
- Never share passwords, your address, or phone number in chats.
- Truth Guard can **make mistakes** — always ask a grown-up when unsure.

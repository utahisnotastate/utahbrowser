# Utah Browser: The Space Station Guide for Kids

**Welcome to your new digital space station!**

Utah Browser is a special browser that keeps your information safe and helps you learn new things with your very own private robot helper.

---

## 1. What is Utah Browser?

Think of it as a **super-safe Clubhouse** on your computer. While other browsers might let strangers track what you're doing, Utah Browser keeps all your secrets on *your* computer.

---

## 2. Your Robot Helper: Truth Guard

Inside the browser, you have a helper called **Truth Guard**. 
- It has a "Brain" that reads your family's notebooks and school notes.
- When you find a sentence on the internet, you can ask Truth Guard: *"Is this true based on my notes?"*
- It will instantly check your notes and give you an answer!

---

## 3. The Cool "Fluidic" Glass Buttons

You might notice that buttons and panels **glow and tilt** when you move your mouse over them. It's like you're touching a magical piece of glowing glass. This helps you see exactly what you're clicking on!

---

## 4. How to be a Pro Explorer

1. **Go Anywhere:** Type a website address at the top and press **Go**.
2. **Fact Check:** Copy a sentence you read, click the **◈** button, and paste it.
3. **Ghost Mode:** If you want to browse without leaving any footprints, click the **Ghost Link** button. It's like having an invisibility cloak!

---

## 5. Staying Safe in Space

- **Talk to a Grown-Up:** Always ask a parent or teacher before visiting new websites.
- **Keep Secrets Secret:** Never tell websites your real name, address, or phone number.
- **Robot Mistakes:** Even smart robots like Truth Guard can be wrong. If something looks weird, ask a grown-up to help you.

---

**Have fun exploring the universe!** 🚀
